## What are cookies

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device. They help a website remember things about your visit.

## Cookies on this site

At the moment, this website does not use tracking or marketing cookies. If that changes, this page will be updated and a cookie banner will ask for your consent first.

## Cookies we may use in future

If we add cookies later, they may include:

- Cookies that remember your preferences
- Cookies that help us understand how visitors use the site

## Managing cookies

Most web browsers let you control cookies through their settings. You can usually find these settings in the "privacy" or "security" section of your browser.
